Engineering Methodology

The Bespoke Commissioning Lifecycle

01

Simulation & Modeling

Finite Element Analysis (FEA) and boundary simulation of horn throats, port geometries, and room reflection interactions.

02

Precision CNC Fabrication

Sub-millimeter 5-axis CNC machining of multi-layer Baltic birch plywood with internal matrix joinery.

03

Anechoic Validation

High-resolution impulse response, polar directivity, and impedance curve verification in our Ljubljana testing lab.

04

On-Site DSP Commissioning

Deploying dedicated FIR phase correction filters and thermal limiter thresholds calibrated to your exact acoustic space.
