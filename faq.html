Loudspeaker System FAQ

Lead times, custom builds, shipping, amplification, rooms and warranty.

  • Ordering
  • Shipping
  • Systems
  • Rooms
  • Warranty & Support

01Ordering

What is the lead time?
Every system is built and calibrated to order in Ljubljana, so lead time depends on the models, quantity and finish. It is confirmed in writing with your quote. If you are working to a fixed date, put it in your enquiry.
Can you build a custom version?
Yes. Custom form factors and matching architectural veneers are available for residential cinemas, mastering suites and performance venues. See Buy and Services for how a bespoke project runs.
Do you sell through dealers?
You can order directly from the lab, or work with one of our authorised integrators in Europe and North America for demonstrations and on-site fitting. Ask us for the partner nearest to you.

02Shipping

Do you ship within the EU?
Yes. Systems ship from Ljubljana, Slovenia, so deliveries inside the EU need no customs clearance. Direct lab orders include secure freight.
Do you ship outside the EU?
Yes, we ship worldwide. Import duties and taxes depend on the destination country.

03Systems

Which amplifier should I use?
The DAP-8 is designed for D&A loudspeakers: eight channels of 2000 W into 4 Ω with full system DSP, enough to run a complete stack from one 2U chassis. If you plan to use existing amplification, send us the model and we will tell you whether it is suitable.
Do the speakers need DSP?
Plan on it. D&A systems are specified with DSP for crossover, alignment and limiting, and the published response figures assume it. Factory presets with FIR phase-linear crossovers and limiter thresholds are supplied for standard DSP platforms, and the DAP-8 carries full system DSP of its own.

04Rooms

What room information do you need?
Room dimensions, existing treatment, listening or audience distance, and the SPL you need at that position. The contact page lists everything that helps us start modelling.
Do you measure on site?
Yes. On-site measurement and DSP calibration for the specific room is part of our services, alongside 3D room modelling and coverage analysis before anything is built.

05Warranty & Support

What does the warranty cover?
Every enclosure, transducer and passive crossover carries a 5-year lab warranty.
What is included with each cabinet?
An individual calibration certificate with that serial number's polar plot and frequency response, factory DSP presets, and direct access to the engineers who designed it for alignment, rigging calculations and tuning. Specification sheets are on the downloads page.

Question not covered?

Write to the engineering team directly. Every enquiry is answered within two working days.
