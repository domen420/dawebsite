Amplification & DSP

DAP-8

8-Channel DSP Power Amplifier

The DAP-8 is D&A Innovations’ custom amplifier platform: eight channels of class-D amplification and full system DSP in a single 2U chassis, sized to drive two complete D&A loudspeakers from one rack space.

Each channel delivers up to 2000 W into 4 Ω, with pairs bridgeable to 3000 W into 8 Ω for subwoofers. Two signal inputs each feed four output channels, so one DAP-8 runs two 4-way cabinets. Crossovers, FIR and IIR filtering, delay and limiters run inside the amplifier, so the approved presets travel with it.

Technical Specifications

Product Type

8-Channel Class-D Power Amplifier with DSP

Channels

2 inputs, 8 outputs (each input feeds 4 outputs); outputs bridgeable in pairs

Power per Channel

2000 W @ 4 Ω / 1000 W @ 8 Ω / 600 W @ 16 Ω

Bridged Power

3000 W @ 8 Ω / 2000 W @ 16 Ω per bridged pair

Max Output Voltage / Current

150 V peak / 54 A peak per channel

Inputs

2x analog XLR with link thru, 2x AES3 XLR, 4x RJ45 network (AES3 over network)

DSP

40-bit floating point, 330 MHz SHARC, 4.02 ms fixed latency

Converters

24-bit Tandem AD/DA; digital inputs 32 – 192 kHz with sample rate conversion

Filters

Custom FIR and parametric IIR; Butterworth, Linkwitz-Riley, Bessel crossovers 6 – 48 dB/oct

Delay

Up to 4 s input delay plus 100 ms output delay

Limiters & Protection

TruePower, RMS and peak limiters; DampingControl; thermal, short-circuit and overload protection

Frequency Response

20 Hz – 20 kHz (+0 / -1 dB)

S/N Ratio

115 dB (A-weighted)

THD+N

< 0.09% from 0.1 W to full power (typically < 0.05%)

Damping Factor

> 500 @ 100 Hz

Mains

100 – 240 V, 50/60 Hz, universal supply with PFC (90 – 264 V operating)

Presets & Control

Up to 10 on-board presets per input, unlimited via network; remote control and monitoring over Ethernet

Outputs

4x speakON NL4 (2 channels each); 2x NL8 (4 channels each) on request

Format

2U, 19-inch (483 mm) rack width, standard amplifier depth

Per-channel power is the maximum for a single channel · subject to final measurement

Engineering & Signal Processing

System Presets Built In

Crossovers, FIR phase correction, up to 4 s of delay and limiters for every D&A loudspeaker run inside the DAP-8. One amplifier replaces a separate processor and keeps each cabinet within its tested limits.

Power for Horns and Subs

150 V peak output voltage and 54 A peak output current per channel keep long-excursion drivers under control, and bridged pairs deliver 3000 W into 8 Ω for single-channel subwoofer loads.

Networked and Redundant

Analog and AES3 inputs, plus a network link that carries digital audio and control. It sets itself up automatically in redundant, daisy-chained layouts, so a failed cable does not interrupt audio or control.

Precision point-source speakers, multiple entry horns, and subwoofers engineered in Ljubljana, Slovenia.
