Multiple Entry Horn

DA610

3-Way Horn-Loaded Loudspeaker

The DA610 is D&A Innovations’ flagship multi-way horn system for high-density live sound, festival stages, nightclubs, and high-output installations.

Six 10" horn-loaded low-frequency drivers, multiple 6.5" midrange drivers, and eight compression drivers are integrated into one large-format constant-directivity waveguide. This 18-driver design performs as a coherent point source, delivering powerful low-mid impact, clear vocals, precise imaging, controlled coverage, and massive dynamic headroom.

Designed for multi-amplified DSP operation, the DA610 delivers exceptional output, efficiency, and definition from a compact enclosure.

Engineering & Waveguide Technology

Co-Entrant Waveguide

Multiple frequency bands enter the flare at acoustically optimized boundary positions where the horn's cross-sectional area matches the acoustic impedance of each driver band, generating a single coherent spherical wavefront.

Extreme Power Density

Packing six 10" high-excursion transducers into a unified horn flare provides tremendous mid-bass impact and sensitivity, outperforming conventional line arrays in phase accuracy and impulse response.

Structural Integrity & Rigging

Constructed from CNC-machined18 mm Baltic birch with internal structural reinforcement. Features integrated multi-point flying tracks and recessed road handles for touring efficiency.

Precision point-source speakers, multiple entry horns, and subwoofers engineered in Ljubljana, Slovenia.
